## Overview

cuelock is an expansion-based net revenue retention (NRR) platform built for customer success managers. It surfaces account signals that indicate expansion opportunities, and nudges CSMs toward the right play at the right time.

## Stack

- **App:** Next.js + Postgres
- **Pipelines:** Python workers for signal ingestion
- **AI:** LLM-backed summarization of call notes and CRM activity
- **Integrations:** Salesforce, HubSpot, Gong
